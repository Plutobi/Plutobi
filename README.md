## Oluwatobi Alabi · Sales Automation for B2B Teams in DACH

I build and run sales automations for small B2B sales teams in Germany, Austria and Switzerland, based in Berlin. Your reps spend their day talking to buyers, and the tools handle the rest:

- **Daily Lead Finder:** ten new people matching your ideal customer every morning, each with a verified email, a fit score out of 100 and a tier from A to D, delivered to a Google Sheet or your CRM.
- **Buying-Signal Alerts in Slack:** every morning, the five companies in your market that are hiring salespeople, raised funding or hired a new sales leader, with the right contact and a first line.
- **Contact Lookup:** a name in Slack or one click on LinkedIn returns the verified email, phone and company details. Contacts found before cost nothing a second time.
- **Lead Cleanup and Scoring:** leads from forms, events and imports arrive cleaned, deduplicated and ranked.

Setups run on the client's own accounts with EU data storage and go live in about a week. Packages start at €250 setup and €75 a month.
